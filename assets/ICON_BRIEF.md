# mathlas — icon brief (for image generation)

A minimalist, modern logo for **mathlas**, a free, no-LLM tool that an AI *uses* to do airtight mathematics — theorem search plus numeric and formal verification. Fuse a clean mathematical glyph — a summation sign ∑, an integral ∫, or a stylized QED tombstone ∎ — with a subtle tool or open-hand motif, conveying "the precise hands an AI lacks." Use a confident two-color palette (deep indigo + one bright accent) on a transparent background, in a flat geometric vector style with no gradients and no 3D. Keep it crisp and readable at favicon size; it should feel rigorous and trustworthy, like a serious developer tool rather than a cartoon.

Deliverables: a 512×512 transparent-PNG logo (square) and a 1280×640 PNG social-preview banner.
